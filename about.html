About Ridhyansh Store

Simple shopping — prepaid UPI, fast dispatch, delivery to your doorstep.

Ridhyansh Store is a small, trusted online store. We want every product's price to be crystal clear, ordering to be easy, and customers to get complete information at every step of the order.

How to place an order

  • Choose a product — from the home page or the product page.
  • Add it to the cart and go to checkout.
  • Enter your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • Pay using the UPI payment link — all orders are prepaid.
  • Send the payment confirmation on WhatsApp; once confirmed, your order is dispatched.

Our commitments

  • ⚡ Fast dispatch — orders are shipped quickly after payment confirmation.
  • 🔒 100% prepaid UPI — you pay exactly the amount shown, nothing more.
  • 💬 WhatsApp support — reach out to us directly with any question.
  • 🚚 Home delivery — provide your complete address and we deliver there.
Have a query? Contact us on WhatsApp from the Contact page.
