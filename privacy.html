Privacy Policy

Your data is kept simple and safe — that's the rule.

What we collect

  • For orders: name, phone number, delivery address and order details.
  • In your browser (localStorage): product and order details are stored in your own browser — our server does not keep them.

Where it goes

  • Orders are stored in the cloud (Firebase) and can be seen only by the store owner.
  • Your phone number is used for WhatsApp order confirmations and support.
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone.

Your rights

  • You can contact us anytime and ask us to delete your data from the Contact page.
This policy may be updated over time. Major changes will be announced on this page.
