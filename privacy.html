Privacy Policy

Aapka data simple aur safe rakhte hain — yeh hi rule hai.

Hum kya collect karte hain

  • Order ke liye: naam, phone number, delivery address, order details.
  • Browser ke andar (localStorage): aapke browser mein hi products/details store hote hain — humara server unhe nahi rakhta.

Kahan jaata hai

  • Orders cloud (Firebase) mein save hote hain, sirf store owner unhe dekh sakte hain.
  • Phone number WhatsApp order confirmation aur support ke liye use hota hai.
  • Hum aapka data kisi se bechte nahi, share nahi karte.

Aapke rights

  • Kisi bhi time Contact karke apna data delete karne ke liye kah sakte hain.
Yeh policy time ke saath update ho sakti hai. Major changes par site par update kiya jata hai.
