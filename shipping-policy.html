Shipping Policy

Order kya hoga, kab bheja jayega — sab kuch transparent.

  • Prepaid only: Saare orders UPI prepaid hain. Dispatch sirf payment confirm hone ke baad.
  • Dispatch: Payment confirm hone ke baad 24 ghante ke andar dispatch karne ki koshish.
  • Shipping charges: Checkout par saaf dikhte hain. Free shipping qualifying amount (usually ₹999+) par available.
  • Delivery: Dispatch ke baad delivery time location par depend karta hai — WhatsApp par updates milti hain.
  • Address: Delivery address mein pincode zaroor likhiye; galat address se delay ho sakta hai.
  • Tracking: Track Order page aur WhatsApp — dono se status milta hai.
